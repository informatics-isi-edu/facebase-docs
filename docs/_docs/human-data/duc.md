---
title: A. Read and complete the Data Use Certification Agreement (DUC)
permalink: /docs/duc/
---

1. Go to the [FaceBase Data Use Certification Agreement (DUC) page](https://www.facebase.org/policies/data-use-certification/), then download and read the PDF.

2. Print the PDF and complete the signature page at the end of the document. Each of the following people must provide their printed name, position at the institution, signature and date:

    - **Principal Investigator (PI)**
    - **Information Technology (IT) Director:** generally a senior IT official at your institution
    - **Institutional Signing Official:** the person with authority to legally bind your institution in administrative matters, typically in its Office of Sponsored Research or equivalent

    These roles are defined in Section 14 of the DUC.

3. Keep the signed DUC. You will submit it with your signed DAR form in [step D]({{ "/docs/print-sign-submit/" | relative_url }}).
