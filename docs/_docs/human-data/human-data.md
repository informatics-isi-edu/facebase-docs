Controlled-access data is available to authorized users for specific research use only after approval by the Data Access Committee (DAC). The request process is described below.

## Before You Begin

Before starting a Data Access Request (DAR), make sure you:

- Confirm you meet eligibility requirements ([see required credentials]({{ "/assets/files/FaceBase-Access-Standards-Required-Credentials-for-Controlled-Access.pdf" | relative_url }}))
- Join the FaceBase Users group
- Prepare the required information (see below)

### Eligibility Requirements

Review the [required credentials]({{ "/assets/files/FaceBase-Access-Standards-Required-Credentials-for-Controlled-Access.pdf" | relative_url }}) to confirm:

- Who is eligible to submit a DAR  
- Institutional signing official requirements  
- Additional roles and definitions described in the Data Use Certification (DUC)

### Join the FaceBase Users Group

To create and submit a DAR, you must join the FaceBase Users group.

1. Click **Sign Up** at the top right corner of the FaceBase site  
   or [use this link](https://app.globus.org/groups/143f5bdc-c127-11e4-ab32-22000a1dd033/about)  
2. Log in with Globus  
3. Request to join the **FaceBase Users** group  

*If you have previously submitted data to FaceBase, you may already be a member.*

### Information You’ll Need

Prepare the following before starting your request.

#### Research Description

- **Research Use Statement**  
  Summary of your research intent, including:
  - Objectives  
  - Study design  
  - Analysis plan  

- **Non-Technical Summary**  
  Brief lay description of your proposed work  

*Note: If your request is approved, these summaries will be publicly viewable.*

#### Compliance and Data Use

- Description of how your research aligns with applicable **Data Use Limitations (DULs)**  
- Description of **data security measures** used to protect downloaded data  

#### Team Information

- List of collaborators (internal and external)

#### IRB Documentation (if required)

If the requested dataset requires IRB approval, you will need:

- IRB letter  
- IRB-approved protocol describing your use of the data  

For questions about IRB requirements, contact the DAC at  
<a href="mailto:nidcrfacebasedac@mail.nih.gov">nidcrfacebasedac@mail.nih.gov</a>  
or see the [IRB FAQ](https://www.facebase.org/help/faqs/#irb)

To access controlled data, follow the steps below:

- [A. Read and complete the Data Use Certification (DUC)]({{ "/docs/duc/" | relative_url }})
- [B. Complete the online Data Access Request (DAR) form]({{ "/docs/dar/" | relative_url }})
- [C. Select the requested datasets]({{ "/docs/select-datasets/" | relative_url }})
- [D. Print, sign, and submit the DUC and DAR forms]({{ "/docs/print-sign-submit/" | relative_url }})
- [E. Await notification]({{ "/docs/notification/" | relative_url }})
- [F. Create or upload an encryption key]({{ "/docs/encryption-keys/" | relative_url }})
- [G. Renew access or submit closeout]({{ "/docs/renewing/" | relative_url }})
