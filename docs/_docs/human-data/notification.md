## After you submit

Your request is reviewed by the NIDCR FaceBase Data Access Committee (DAC), which may ask you to revise it. Once your request is processed and reviewed, you will be notified via email whether or not your request is approved, and the *Status* of your DAR will change from "Completed" to "Approved" or "Rejected".

Allow approximately 4-6 weeks for a response.

## Access your data

If your request is approved, your FaceBase login account provides access to the approved datasets. To access the data:

1. Go to the page for your DAR, using the link in your approval email, and log in using the same credential you used to create the DAR.
2. Scroll down to the *Requested Datasets* section and choose one of the options below.

### Download individual files

Select the desired dataset from the list, scroll down to the *File* section of that dataset, and click on a file to download it to your computer.

### Download all files for one or more datasets in your DAR using a BDBag

A BDBag file contains metadata and information on how to download the data files, but not the data files themselves.

Click the RID to open the desired dataset or, if you want to download all the files in all the datasets in your DAR, click **Explore** to the right of the *Requested Datasets* section.

[**QUESTION: There is no Explore button to the right of the *Requested Datasets* section.**]

Then click **Export** at the top right corner and select **BDBag**. This downloads a `dataset.zip` file to your computer. ]

To download the data files, follow the instructions in [Exporting Data from FaceBase]({{ "/docs/Exporting-Data-from-FaceBase/" | relative_url }}).

**Be cautious:** downloading the files from a BDBag may result in a very large amount of data being downloaded to your machine.

## Your access period and responsibilities

You will have access to the human data for **one year after the approval date**. Under the [FaceBase Data Use Certification Agreement (DUC)](https://www.facebase.org/policies/data-use-certification/), you also agree to:

- [Renew your access or close out your project]({{ "/docs/renewing/" | relative_url }}) before the one-year access period ends. Either one includes a progress update, and closing out includes destroying the data.
- Use the data only for the research described in your approved DAR.
- Report any data security incidents or violations of the terms of access within 24 hours of identifying them, as described in Sections 7 and 8 of the DUC.
- Acknowledge the data in your presentations and publications, as described in Section 10 of the DUC.

This is only a summary. See the DUC for the full terms.

## Questions

If you have any questions regarding this process or need help with downloads please contact the FaceBase Help Desk via email: **help AT facebase.org**.
