On the DAR form you created in the previous step, you will select the desired datasets.

If you are returning to a DAR you saved earlier, go to the [Data Access Request (DAR) page](https://www.facebase.org/chaise/recordset/#1/isa:data_access_request), log in, and find your request by its title or Principal Investigator.

## Add each dataset you are requesting

Scroll down to the *Requested Datasets* section of the page and click on the **+ Link records** button to open the Datasets selection page.

![The Requested Datasets section of the DAR form with the + Link records button]({{ "/assets/img/dar-select-datasets-1.png" | relative_url }})

## Searching for controlled access datasets

If you know details of the datasets like Title, Description, etc, you can search by entering this text in the *Search all columns* field at the top left of the page. To narrow your search to human-subject datasets, click on the **Show filter panel** link to open the filters panel:

![The Datasets selection page with the Show filter panel link]({{ "/assets/img/dar-select-datasets-2.png" | relative_url }})

Then select "Yes" on the Protected Human Subjects filter to show all datasets that contain protected human data. You can continue narrowing your search by using the other filters like Experiment Type, Gene, Syndrome, Anatomy, etc, and then pick the desired datasets from the results set shown on the right by clicking on each dataset checkbox.

### Finding EarBase datasets

If you are requesting access to EarBase datasets, you can find them quickly by opening the *Consortium* filter in the filters panel and choosing **EarBase**.

![The Consortium filter with EarBase selected]({{ "/assets/img/dar-select-datasets-earbase.png" | relative_url }})

## Save the form

Once you are done picking all your datasets, click on the **Save** button on the top right corner of the page.

![The Datasets selection page with datasets selected and the Save button at the top right]({{ "/assets/img/dar-select-datasets-3.png" | relative_url }})

The list of selected datasets appears under the *Requested Datasets* section of the DAR form. You may add new datasets to this list by repeating this process. After your DAR is approved, adding new datasets to the project requires an amendment to your request.

![The DAR form with the selected datasets listed under Requested Datasets]({{ "/assets/img/dar-select-datasets-4.png" | relative_url }})

## Removing datasets from the request

Remove datasets from this list by clicking on the **Unlink records** button to the right of the *Requested Datasets* section and selecting the datasets to remove.

![The Requested Datasets section with the Unlink records button]({{ "/assets/img/dar-select-datasets-5.png" | relative_url }})
